## Open your profile from a workspace

Select your avatar in the top navigation, then choose **Account settings**. **Profile** shows your personal account details.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/account-settings-menu.jpg" alt="Account settings in the avatar menu."><figcaption><p>Open your profile from the account menu.</p></figcaption></figure>

If the workspace name stays in the top navigation while you view **Profile**, select a workspace area there to return to your work. An older profile bookmark that doesn’t name a workspace opens in your active workspace on this layout.
