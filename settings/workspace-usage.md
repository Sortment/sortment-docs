# Understand your workspace usage

Use **Workspace settings → Billing → Plan** to check the usage and allowances for the selected workspace. Under **Usage this month**, review **Emails**, **AI tokens**, **Seats**, **Data connections**, **Active quests**, and **Active tasks**.

## Read the counters

| Counter | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Emails** | Email volume recorded for campaign and journey sends. It is a sending counter, not a count of delivered messages or unique recipients. |
| **AI tokens** | AI usage attributed to the workspace, including work performed by agents. A token allowance is not a number of chats or Tasks. |
| **AI tokens (one-time grant)** | An allowance measured against the workspace's lifetime AI usage rather than a monthly reset. |
| **Seats**, **Data connections**, **Active quests**, **Active tasks** | Current usage of the corresponding workspace resources. These are not monthly activity totals. |

Check the included amount beside each counter for your workspace's plan. A billing counter and a campaign delivery report answer different questions: use [campaign reports](../engage/campaigns/campaign-reports/glossary.md) to investigate delivery and engagement.

## When do email and AI allowances reset?

Monthly allowances follow the workspace's billing day, including on quarterly and annual payment terms. For example, a billing day of the 15th gives monthly usage periods starting on the 15th; it does not provide one annual usage pool. If the billing day falls beyond the end of a shorter month, that month's period starts on the last day. Period boundaries use UTC.

Without a paid subscription, the billing account's creation day supplies the monthly boundary. A workspace without a billing account uses calendar months. An AI allowance labelled **one-time grant** does not reset monthly.

## What if I see an allowance error?

If Sortment reports that your email allowance or AI credits are used up, check the current workspace and its **Plan** tab. Ask a workspace admin or Billing member to review the available plan options. If the count or allowance looks wrong, [contact support](../getting-started/contact-us.md) with the workspace name and the displayed error.

A displayed overage rate does not by itself mean another send or AI request will be allowed. Follow the actual error and your workspace's plan terms. After resolving a limit, check the campaign or Task's state before retrying; a plan change is not confirmation that interrupted work has resumed.
