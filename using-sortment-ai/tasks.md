# Tasks

A Task gives Sortment a defined piece of work to perform on a schedule or trigger. Use a Task for a repeatable check, such as reviewing campaign performance each morning and reporting what needs attention.

## Create a Task from a template

1. On the home page, open **Chats** and switch to **Tasks**.
2. Select **New task** to open the template picker.
3. Choose a template, or **Start with a blank task**.
4. Enter a name, such as **Daily campaign review**, and select **Create task**.
5. Finish the brief in the side chat that opens. Specify what to check, the schedule or trigger, the time zone, relevant campaigns or references, and where you want the findings.
6. Review the agreed brief and schedule before asking Sortment to enable the Task. Check that the Task is active when you are ready for it to run.

Selecting **Create task** creates a saved, inactive Task and starts the setup conversation. It does not mean the Task is scheduled to run.

A newly created Task can initially show a provisional one-time schedule. Replace it with your intended schedule during setup. Choosing a template supplies a starting brief, not a final schedule or permission to take every action it suggests.

## Create a Task through chat

You can also describe the work directly to Sortment:

> Create a Task to review my active campaigns every weekday at 9 AM America/New_York. Report issues and suggested next steps. Do not change the campaigns. Keep the Task inactive until I approve the brief and schedule.

Answer the clarifying questions, review the resulting Task, and enable it when ready. Be explicit about whether you want a report, suggestions, or actions, and which resources the Task should use.

## Read a Task's report

Open **Chats → Tasks** to find your Tasks and their runs. Select a run to read its report.

When a run produces an Alert or Insight on the home page, open it and select **Explore** to ask follow-up questions with that run's context.

## Why hasn't a new Task run?

Check whether the Task is active and whether its agreed schedule or trigger has been reached. A saved name and a completed setup conversation are not substitutes for checking the Task's current state.

If the schedule is still provisional, finish setup before enabling it. If the Task reports an error, use that error to investigate; do not repeatedly enable or recreate the Task.

## How is Quest setup different?

From **Plan → Quests → New**, choose a template or **Start with a blank quest**, name it, and select **Create quest** to open the Quest and its setup chat. The chat helps you refine the objective and brief.

Email warm-up templates have an additional enrollment step after naming. Review the sender, provider, and enrollment action before starting them; they can create provider resources and begin warm-up.

See [Quest, Task, or Journey?](../faq/quest-task-or-journey.md) to choose the right workflow.
