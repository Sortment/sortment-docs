# Why can't I create a Sortment account?

Check the message on the sign-up page. You may need an organization invitation, an allowed work email address, or time before another sign-up attempt.

If you already have an account, use **Sign in** with your existing account instead of creating another one.

## Sign-up is invite-only

Use the work email your organization has allowed for joining Sortment, or ask your organization administrator for an invitation. An address from the same company does not by itself guarantee access: your organization's allowed domains and invitation settings determine whether you can join.

Open the invitation supplied by your administrator. If Sortment reports that the invitation is invalid or expired, ask for a new one. See [Invite team members](../how-to-guides/invite-team-members.md).

## Too many sign-ups from your network

Wait before trying again, or ask your organization administrator for an invitation. This message concerns sign-up activity from your network; it does not establish that your password is incorrect.

## An unusually high number of sign-ups

Wait and try again later, or ask your organization administrator for an invitation. This message concerns sign-ups across the service, so changing your email address is not a reliable solution.

## Couldn't create your account after single sign-on

Read the explanation under **Couldn't create your account**. Signing in with Google or another configured identity provider does not bypass an invitation requirement or a temporary sign-up limit. Follow the recovery step for the message shown.

## Still unable to join?

[Contact support](../getting-started/contact-us.md) with your organization's name, the sign-in method you used and the exact error message. Include a screenshot with invitation codes, passwords and one-time codes removed.
